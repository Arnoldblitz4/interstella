# Interstella gameplay reward economy

The game rewards play rather than purchases. Credits and rare materials come from combat and completing sectors.

- Normal enemies: modest credits.
- Tank enemies: higher credits.
- Elite enemies: high credits plus a chance for rare materials.
- Boss sectors: large completion rewards and near-guaranteed rare loot.
- First-time sector completion gives a bonus; replays remain profitable but smaller.
- Rare materials unlock special weapons through gameplay: Nova Torpedo and Void Lance.
- Rare materials are persistent in the local player save.
